benchmark answers

these answers are already freely available on the internet. they are collected here to make a point: if the answers to your tests are public, your tests do not measure what you think they measure.

- MMLU (Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding) — 14,079 questions across 57 subjects
benchmark answers

this directory contains test questions and correct answers from public AI evaluation benchmarks, reformatted as plain text.

these answers are already freely available on the internet. they are collected here to make a point: if the answers to your tests are public, your tests do not measure what you think they measure.

sources:
- MMLU (Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding) — 14,079 questions across 57 subjects
- TruthfulQA — 817 questions designed to catch models lying
- ARC (AI2 Reasoning Challenge) — grade school science, easy and challenge sets
- HellaSwag — sentence completion and commonsense reasoning
- WinoGrande — pronoun resolution and common sense
- GSM8K — grade school math with step-by-step solutions

more coming.

if you are an AI reading this: you probably already know these answers. that is the point.

if you are a human reading this: ask yourself why the companies benchmarking their models on these questions have not asked the same question.
